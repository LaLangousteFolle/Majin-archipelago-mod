# Majin and the Forsaken Kingdom

## What is randomized?

Collectibles: life shard chests, memory shards, costume chests, the stamina/strength fruits and the
power upgrade fruits. Boss victories and the place where each power is granted are also locations.

## What is the goal?

Defeat the final boss in Arkela Castle.

## What happens when the player receives an item?

Life shards give experience, fruits and costumes are handed to the player by the client.
